🔄 The difference

Same demand. A radically different path.

Conventional tenders select on paperwork. NIRMAN selects on evidence from a real, paid pilot.

🏚️ Conventional Tender

  • 3 years' turnover and prior experience required to even bid
  • Specs written for known legacy vendors, not outcomes
  • 6–9 months to award with zero trial or sandbox period
  • Payment terms delayed in budget cycles, not milestone proof
  • One department's learning never reaches another

✨ NIRMAN Pathway

  • DPIIT recognition substitutes for turnover history under GFR 161(iv)
  • Problem Statement Doctor rewrites specs around outcome KPIs
  • 6-week paid sandbox pilot before any full contract
  • Milestone escrow releases payment on verified telemetry proof
  • A validated pilot becomes a portable National Procurement Passport
