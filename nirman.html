Why We Built NIRMAN

The missing middle in Indian public procurement

In public procurement across Indian states, officials face a classic double-bind. If an officer contracts an untested startup and it fails, audit agencies cite GFR violations for bypassing 3-year turnover thresholds. If they play it safe and stick to legacy L1 vendor catalogs, PHCs and municipal wards keep running on decades-old manual logs. We built NIRMAN to institutionalize the missing middle: a legally sound, milestone-escrowed pilot sandbox governed by GFR 2017 Rule 173(i) and independent STQC verification, giving honest officers the audit trail they need to procure frontier tech without personal career risk.

GFR 2017 Rule 173(i) — Startup Turnover Waiver

General Financial Rules, Government of India

Rule Text (Paraphrased)

"Prior experience and prior turnover shall not be a criterion for recognising DPIIT-certified startups as eligible bidders, provided quality and technical standards are satisfied."

NIRMAN Statutory Framework Matrix (GFR 2017)
GFR Rule Statutory Purpose Stage Applied
Rule 173(i) DPIIT Turnover & Prior Experience Waiver for innovative startups Screening & Sandbox Entry
Rule 161(iv) Interstate sandbox adoption & reciprocal fast-track procurement Scale Decision & Interstate
Rule 149 GeM PAC direct procurement & departmental marketplace catalog Scaleup Marketplace

Why NIRMAN cites this rule: Every challenge on the NIRMAN platform is posted as an outcome-based challenge (not an L1 tender). A startup applying for a pilot needs only a valid DPIIT certificate — no 3-year balance sheet, no prior government contract history.

Supplementary: Ministry of Finance OM F.20/2/2014-PPD(Pt.) extends this waiver to all Central and State procurement procedures where DPIIT recognition is verified. NIRMAN's automated screening gate verifies certificate numbers directly against the DPIIT registry schema.

Milestone Escrow Protocol

How NIRMAN disburses pilot funds

1. Startup submits evidence — telemetry logs, field officer sign-off, and sensor readings uploaded via Evidence Modal. Escrow remains locked.
2. Independent Validator reviews — STQC/IIT-empanelled auditor checks three items: evidence reviewed, matches KPI target, no discrepancies. Only they can confirm.
3. Escrow disbursed — only after validator confirms. Government nodal officer triggers PFMS release. SHA-256 audit hash recorded.

STQC Directorate Validation Norms

Standardisation Testing and Quality Certification (MeitY, GoI)

Statutory Role in NIRMAN

STQC acts as the sovereign third-party telemetry and evidence validation authority. Under MeitY directives, STQC empanelled auditors verify raw IoT data streams, algorithmic integrity, and milestone achievement before public funds are released.

Auditor Empanelment & Sign-Off Criteria:

  • Telemetry Integrity: Cryptographic SHA-256 validation of raw sensor logs against physical edge calibrators.
  • Field Baseline Verification: Physical inspection of pilot deployment sites (e.g. 12 PHCs in Barabanki).
  • Credential Binding: Every release report is cryptographically sealed by an empanelled auditor (e.g. Prof. Dr. M. Sundaram, IIT Delhi / STQC Ref: VAL-9042).
