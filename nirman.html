🔄 The difference

Same demand. A radically different path.

Conventional tenders select on paperwork. NIRMAN selects on evidence from a real, paid pilot.

🏚️

Conventional Tender

  • 3 years' turnover required to even bid
  • Specs written for known vendors, not outcomes
  • 6–9 months to award, no trial period
  • Payment terms set after contract, not before
  • One department's learning never reaches another
✨

NIRMAN Pathway

  • DPIIT recognition substitutes for turnover history
  • Problem Statement Doctor rewrites specs around outcomes
  • 6-week sandbox pilot before any full contract
  • Milestone escrow releases payment on verified proof
  • A validated pilot becomes a portable passport
⚙️ The process

Eight stages, one thread

Every challenge moves through the same auditable sequence — from problem statement to district-wide scale. Each step is logged, auditable, and carries forward to the next.
